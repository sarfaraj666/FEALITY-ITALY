KNOW FEALITY

Frequently Asked Questions · FEALITY ITALY

1. What makes Feality Italy kitchens different from conventional wooden kitchens?

Feality Italy redefines modern kitchens with premium 304-grade stainless steel, Italian-inspired aesthetics and thoughtful functionality. Unlike conventional wooden kitchens, stainless steel offers excellent resistance to moisture, termites and everyday kitchen challenges, making it a practical choice for modern homes.

2. Why should I choose a stainless steel kitchen over a wooden kitchen?

A kitchen is a long-term investment. Stainless steel offers durability, moisture resistance, termite resistance and easy maintenance. It combines lasting performance with contemporary design, helping you create a kitchen that looks beautiful and works efficiently for years.

3. Are Feality Italy kitchens suitable for Indian cooking?

Absolutely! Feality kitchens are designed with modern living in mind, while considering the practical requirements of Indian households. From everyday cooking to elaborate meals, stainless steel provides a durable and easy-to-maintain kitchen environment.

4. What is 304-grade stainless steel, and why does it matter?

304-grade stainless steel is a widely used stainless steel grade known for its corrosion resistance, durability and suitability for demanding environments. It offers an excellent material foundation for kitchen applications where hygiene, moisture and everyday use matter.

5. Are stainless steel kitchens available in different designs and finishes?

Yes! Modern stainless steel kitchens are not limited to a single industrial appearance. Feality Italy offers design possibilities through different colours, finishes, laminates, glass, paint and other design elements, bringing elegance and individuality to your kitchen.

6. Are Feality Italy kitchens easy to maintain?

Yes. Stainless steel surfaces are generally easy to clean and maintain with appropriate cleaning products and regular care. Their resistance to moisture and termites also helps reduce some common maintenance concerns associated with conventional wooden kitchens.

7. Can I customise my kitchen according to my space and preferences?

Yes! Every home has its own character. Your kitchen can be planned around available space, layout requirements, preferred finishes, storage needs and design preferences to create a personalised kitchen experience.

8. Does FEALITY ITALY offer a lifetime kitchen warranty?

Yes. We offer a lifetime warranty on our stainless steel kitchens. When planning your kitchen, ask our team which components are covered and how to make a claim. Ask about the warranty.
