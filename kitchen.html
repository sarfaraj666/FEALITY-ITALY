Know FEALITY ITALY

1. Which collections can I explore?

Browse the Kitchen, Wardrobes and Bathroom Vanity collections. Each page includes featured spaces, detail views and a full gallery.

2. How do I compare kitchen designs?

Open Explore Every Design on the Kitchen page. CURVIERA, MORBIDA and ONYX are the first three designs. Select a photograph to see the original at a larger size.

3. Where can I see wardrobe storage or basin details?

Visit wardrobe details for storage compositions, or vanity details for mirrors, basins and cabinetry.

4. Can I browse photos on my phone?

Open a photograph and swipe horizontally, or use the previous and next buttons. The counter shows your position in the gallery.

5. Can I browse using my keyboard?

Tab to a photograph and press Enter. Use the arrow keys to move between designs and Escape to close the viewer.

6. What should I prepare for a design conversation?

Keep the names of the designs you like, approximate room measurements and a short list of storage or layout priorities. See How It Works for a guide.

7. Can I order from the gallery?

The galleries are for design exploration. Online ordering and enquiry submission are currently unavailable. See the policy status for commercial information.
